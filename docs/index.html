StyleMatter lab

Four throwaway experiments testing whether presentation can be authored as objects, links, boundaries, and physical constraints instead of property text.

Experiment 01

Properties as connections

Linked material, spring spacing, and inherited style flow with a local gate.

 Experiment 02

Touch the thing

Surface, corner, inner-edge, and inter-object controls revealed where their effects happen.

 Experiment 03

Things like this

Structural selection by pointing at an example and pulling its boundary through the hierarchy.

 Experiment 04

Squeeze the world

Continuous responsive relationships and a movable stop for topology changes.
Persistence intentionally deferred until the interaction model earns it.
